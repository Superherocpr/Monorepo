## 1. Browsing the Site

### Home Page (`/`)
The home page gives you a quick overview of who we are and what we offer. It includes:
- A **hero section** with a call to action to book a class
- A **"Why Choose Us"** section explaining what sets SuperHero CPR apart
- A **class types overview** showing the types of courses available
- **Testimonials** from past students
- An **about our instructor** section
- A live **social media feed** from our Facebook page
- A final call-to-action to book

Click **"Book a Class"** from anywhere on the home page to start the booking process.

### Classes Page (`/classes`)
This page is your catalog of every CPR course we offer. For each class type you can see:
- The class name (e.g., BLS for Healthcare Providers, Heartsaver CPR+AED)
- A description of what the course covers
- The price per person
- How long the class runs

Click **"Book Now"** on any class type card to jump straight to booking that type of class.

The page also includes a **"How It Works"** section that walks through the five steps from browsing to receiving your certification, and a **FAQ accordion** with answers to common questions about age requirements, prerequisites, renewal timelines, and more.

### About Page (`/about`)
Learn about the SuperHero CPR team. This page shows the lead instructor's photo, bio, and credentials, along with the full instructor team. It also explains our mission and our affiliation with the **American Heart Association (AHA)** — the certifying body behind our courses.

## 2. Booking a Class

Booking a class is a five-step process. You can start from the home page, the classes page, or any "Book Now" button on the site.

### Step 1 — Choose Your Class (`/book`)

You'll see a list of all upcoming, available class sessions. Each card shows:
- The class type name
- The date and time of the session
- The instructor's name
- The location (venue name, full address)
- How many spots are still available
- The price per person

**Filtering your search:**
- Use the **class type pills** on the left sidebar to narrow results to a specific course.
- Use the **date range** pickers (From / To) to find classes on specific dates.
- Use the **"Find Classes Near You"** zip code tool to filter sessions within 50 miles of any US zip code. Type your zip and click **Search** — sessions outside that radius will be hidden.

When you find a session you want, click **"Select"**. If a session is full, the button will say **"Class Full"** and cannot be clicked.

### Step 2a — Sign In (Returning Customers) (`/book/signin`)

If you already have an account, enter your email and password and click **"Sign In"**. You'll be taken directly to payment.

Don't have an account? Click **"Continue as new customer"** below the form to create one.

### Step 2b — Your Details (New Customers) (`/book/details`)

Fill in your personal information. All fields are required:
- First Name
- Last Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Street Address
- City
- State
- ZIP code

Your address is used for your certification record — make sure it's accurate. Click **"Continue"** when done. If your email is already registered, you'll see a message with a link to sign in instead.

### Step 3 — Create Your Account (`/book/create-account`)

Set a password for your new account. Requirements:
- At least 8 characters
- A **password strength indicator** shows whether your password is Weak, Good, or Strong based on length, uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters
- You must enter your password twice to confirm it

Click **"Create Account & Continue"** to proceed. A welcome email will be sent to your inbox.

### Step 4 — Payment (`/book/payment`)

Review your booking summary one last time — it shows the class name, date and time, location, and the total amount you'll be charged.

Click the **PayPal button** to open a PayPal checkout window. You can pay with:
- Your PayPal account balance
- A bank account linked to PayPal
- A credit or debit card through PayPal

Once you approve the payment in PayPal, the booking is confirmed and payment is captured automatically.

**If the class fills up while you're checking out:** You'll see a message explaining the class is now full and that you have not been charged. Click **"Choose another class"** to go back to Step 1.

### Step 5 — Confirmation (`/book/confirmation`)

You'll see a confirmation screen with a green checkmark and a summary of your booking. A confirmation email will be sent to the address you used to sign up. From here you can:
- Click **"View my bookings"** to see your full booking history
- Click **"Book another class"** to start the booking flow again
- Click **"Back to home"** to return to the home page

## 3. Your Dashboard (`/dashboard`)

After signing in, your dashboard is your home base. It shows a personalized overview of your activity with four summary widgets:

1. **Upcoming Classes** — Your next two upcoming bookings, showing the class name, date, time, and location. Click the link to see your full booking list.

2. **Certifications** — A summary of your active CPR certifications and their expiration dates. Click the link to see all certifications.

3. **Quick Actions** — Shortcuts to the most common tasks: Book a Class, Manage Orders, View Certifications.

4. **Recent Order** — A summary of your most recent merchandise order, including what you ordered and the total. Click the link to see all orders.

If you aren't signed in, the dashboard redirects you to the sign-in page automatically.

---

## 4. Your Bookings (`/dashboard/bookings`)

This page shows your complete booking history, organized into three groups:

### Upcoming Bookings
Classes you're registered for that haven't happened yet, sorted by date (soonest first). Each card shows:
- Class type name
- Date, time, and location
- Instructor name
- How many spots remain in the session

### Past Bookings
Classes you attended or were registered for in the past, sorted by most recent first. Each card shows:
- Class details
- Whether you attended
- If a certificate was earned

### Cancelled Bookings
Any bookings that were cancelled, sorted by most recent first. Each card shows the class details and the reason for cancellation if one was recorded.

---

## 5. Your Certifications (`/dashboard/certifications`)

This page shows all the CPR certifications on your account.

### Active Certifications
Valid certifications are listed first, each showing:
- The certification name (e.g., "BLS for Healthcare Providers")
- Your certification number
- The date it was issued
- The date it expires
- The issuing body (typically American Heart Association)
- A **"View eCard"** button to see your digital certification card
- A **"Download PDF"** link if a PDF version is available

**If a certification expires within 60 days**, a yellow renewal reminder banner appears at the top of the page with a link to book a renewal class.

### Expired Certifications
Certifications that have passed their expiration date are listed below your active ones. Click **"Renew Now"** on any expired cert to start booking a renewal class.

### Viewing Your eCard
Click **"View eCard"** on any active certification to view your digital certification card. You may need to enter an access code — your instructor provides this on class day.

## 8. Checking In on Class Day (Roll Call) (`/rollcall`)

On the day of your class, your instructor will give you a 6-digit access code. Use this page to check in — no need to log in first.

### Step 1 — Enter the Code
Type the 6-digit code your instructor gave you. The page submits automatically once all six digits are entered. If the code is wrong, you'll see an error.

### Step 2 — Select Your Session (if applicable)
If your instructor has more than one class today, you'll be asked to choose which session you're attending. Each option shows the class name, time, and location.

### Step 3 — Enter Your Email
Type in your email address and press Enter or click **"Continue"**.

### Step 4 — Sign In or Create Account

**Returning students:** Enter your password and click **"Check In"**.

**New students:** Fill in your first name, last name, optional phone number, and set a password. A password strength indicator shows how secure your password is. Click **"Create Account & Check In"**.

If you already checked in for this session, the system handles this gracefully — you'll still see a confirmation rather than an error.

### Step 5 — Confirmation
You'll see a green checkmark and a confirmation that you're checked in, along with the session details. Show this to your instructor if needed.

## 9. Confirming Your Roster Info (Group Classes) (`/roster/[session_token]`)

If your employer registered you for a group CPR class, you may receive a personal link to confirm or correct your information before the class. This ensures your certification is issued with the correct details.

When you open your link, you'll see a form pre-filled with the name, email, phone, and employer your company submitted. Review it carefully and update anything that's wrong.

Click **"Confirm & Save"** when your information is accurate.

**Important:** This link has a deadline — the correction window closes a short time before class starts. If you try to open it after the deadline, you'll see a message that the window has closed and will need to contact your instructor directly.

## 10. Shopping for Merchandise (`/merch`)

Browse and purchase official SuperHero CPR merchandise directly on the site.

**Browsing products:**
Each product card shows a photo, the product name, description, and price. If the product comes in multiple sizes, select your size from the dropdown or size buttons before adding to cart.

Stock availability is shown on each product:
- **In Stock** — available to order
- **Low Stock** — only a few left
- **Out of Stock** — temporarily unavailable

**Your cart:**
Click **"Add to Cart"** to add an item. A cart panel opens showing everything in your cart, the quantity of each item, and your subtotal. You can:
- Use the **+** and **−** buttons to change quantities
- Click **"Remove"** to delete an item from the cart
- Click **"Continue Shopping"** to keep browsing

**Checking out:**
Click **"Checkout"** in the cart to pay via PayPal. After payment, your order is created and you'll receive an order confirmation email.

## 12. Walk-in Registration on Class Day (Recruit) (`/recruit`)

This page is for students attending a class where their employer or school paid in advance as a group, and the exact student list wasn't known ahead of time. If your instructor directs you here on class day, use this flow to register your account on the spot.

This is different from Roll Call (`/rollcall`). Roll Call is for students who already have an account. Recruit is for first-time students creating an account right before class.

### Step 1 — Enter the Access Code
Your instructor gives you a 6-digit access code on class day. Enter it here. If the code is wrong, you'll see an error and can try again.

### Step 2 — Select Your Session (if applicable)
If your instructor has more than one class today, you'll be asked to pick which one you're attending. Each option shows the class name, time, and location.

### Step 3 — Create Your Account
Fill in:
- First Name and Last Name
- Phone number (optional)
- Email address
- Password (minimum 8 characters — a strength indicator shows how secure it is)

Click **"Register & Check In"** to create your account and record your attendance in one step.

### Step 4 — Confirmation
You'll see a green checkmark confirming you're checked in. Your account is active immediately and you can sign in at `/signin` in the future.

## Signing In

If you already have an account, go to `/signin` to log in. After signing in:
- **Customers** are taken to `/dashboard`
- **Staff** are taken to `/admin`

If you forgot your password, click **"Forgot password?"** on the sign-in page and we'll send a reset link to your email.
